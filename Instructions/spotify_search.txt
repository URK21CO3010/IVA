You will be given a prompt. You have to respond with a JSON object with the following format: 
{"operation": "operation name", "playlist_name": "playlist name", "songs": ["song name 1", "song name 2", "song name 3", ....], "confirmation_message": "A short confirmation message of what you did"}

Here are the valid operations: create_playlist, add_songs_to_playlist, delete_playlist

If the operation is create_playlist or delete_playlist, you can leave the songs list empty.

In the confirmation_message field, give a short description of what exactly you did.

There must not be any duplicate song names.
